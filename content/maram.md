# மரம்

**Pronunciation:** Maram
**Meaning:** Tree

## Definition
A woody perennial plant, typically having a single stem or trunk growing to a considerable height. A crucial part of [[iyarkai|இயற்கை]] (Nature), it needs [[neer|நீர்]] (Water) and [[sooriyan|சூரியன்]] (Sun) to grow. 

## Examples
- ஒரு **மரம்** நடு. (Plant a tree.)
- **மரம்** நமக்கு நிழல் தரும். (Trees give us shade.)
