# இயற்கை

**Pronunciation:** Iyarkai
**Meaning:** Nature

## Definition
The phenomena of the physical world collectively, including plants like [[maram|மரம்]] (Tree), animals, the landscape, and other features and products of the earth. Protected by [[neer|நீர்]] (Water) and [[sooriyan|சூரியன்]] (Sun). It brings [[magizhchi|மகிழ்ச்சி]] (Happiness) to humans.

## Examples
- **இயற்கை** மிகவும் அழகானது. (Nature is very beautiful.)
- **இயற்கை** வளங்களை காப்போம். (Let us protect natural resources.)
