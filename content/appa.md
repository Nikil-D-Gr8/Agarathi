# அப்பா

**Pronunciation:** Appa
**Meaning:** Father

## Definition
The male parent of a child. Along with [[amma|அம்மா]] (Mother), they provide [[kalvi|கல்வி]] (Education) and guidance to the children.

## Examples
- என் **அப்பா** ஒரு ஆசிரியர். (My father is a teacher.)
- **அப்பா** வேலைக்குச் செல்கிறார். (Father is going to work.)
