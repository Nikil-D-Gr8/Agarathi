# சூரியன்

**Pronunciation:** Sooriyan
**Meaning:** Sun

## Definition
The star around which the earth orbits. It provides light to the [[ulagam|உலகம்]] (World) during the day, unlike the [[chandiran|சந்திரன்]] (Moon) which appears at night. Essential for [[iyarkai|இயற்கை]] (Nature).

## Examples
- காலையில் **சூரியன்** உதிக்கும். (The sun rises in the morning.)
- **சூரியன்** வெப்பத்தை தரும். (The sun gives heat.)
