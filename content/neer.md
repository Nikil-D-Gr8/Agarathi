# நீர்

**Pronunciation:** Neer
**Meaning:** Water

## Definition
A colorless, transparent, odorless liquid that forms the seas, lakes, rivers, and rain and is the basis of the fluids of living organisms. Essential for [[maram|மரம்]] (Trees) and all life in the [[ulagam|உலகம்]] (World).

## Examples
- **நீர்** இன்றி அமையாது உலகு. (The world cannot exist without water.)
- சுத்தமான **நீர்** குடிக்கவும். (Drink clean water.)
