# உலகம்

**Pronunciation:** Ulagam
**Meaning:** World

## Definition
The earth, together with all of its countries, peoples, and natural features like [[maram|மரம்]] (Tree) and [[neer|நீர்]] (Water). It receives light from the [[sooriyan|சூரியன்]] (Sun).

## Examples
- இந்த **உலகம்** மிகவும் பெரியது. (This world is very big.)
- **உலகம்** உருண்டையானது. (The world is round.)
