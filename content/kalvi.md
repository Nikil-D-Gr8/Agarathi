# கல்வி

**Pronunciation:** Kalvi
**Meaning:** Education, Knowledge

## Definition
The process of receiving or giving systematic instruction, which is essential for understanding the [[ulagam|உலகம்]] (World). It brings true [[magizhchi|மகிழ்ச்சி]] (Happiness).

## Examples
- **கல்வி** ஒரு கண் போன்றது. (Education is like an eye.)
- ஏழைகளுக்கு **கல்வி** கொடு. (Give education to the poor.)
