# அம்மா

**Pronunciation:** Amma
**Meaning:** Mother

## Definition
The woman who gives birth to a child, or a mother figure. A primary source of [[anbu|அன்பு]] (Love) and care. Usually mentioned along with [[appa|அப்பா]] (Father).

## Examples
- என் **அம்மா** என்னை நேசிக்கிறார். (My mother loves me.)
- **அம்மா** சமைக்கிறார். (Mother is cooking [[unavu|உணவு]].)
